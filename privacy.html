Privacy Policy Stream Stats is a private tool used only by its owner with the owner's own YouTube channel. It requests read-only access (youtube.readonly) to display live-stream statistics: concurrent viewers, likes and broadcast identifiers. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data to third parties. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sent anywhere except to Google's API. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
